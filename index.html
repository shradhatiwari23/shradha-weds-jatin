How to Reach Rishikesh

By Air

Fly into Jolly Grant Airport, Dehradun (DED) (approx. 40 km from the venue). Pre-booked shuttles & taxis are readily available to Mohan Chatti.

By Train

Arrive at Haridwar Junction (HW) or Yog Nagari Rishikesh (YNRK). The resort is an easy scenic 1-hour mountain drive from the station.

By Road

An approximate 5 to 6-hour smooth journey from Delhi-NCR via the Delhi-Meerut Expressway and NH 334 through Meerut, Muzaffarnagar & Haridwar.

Winter Note: Rishikesh experiences pleasant sunny afternoons (~20°C) and brisk, chilly evenings (~7°C) in late January. Please carry warm shawls, jackets, and formal winter layers.
